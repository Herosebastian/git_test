This page covers common images formats, but you may also be looking for links and useful extras

JPGs

JPG images are good for photots as they compress color well but cannot render transparent pixels.

GIFs

GIFs are goof for displaying animations with little detail as they portray detail and color poorly

PNGs

PNG's are more detailed but space heavy JPEGs that can handle transparent pixels

SVGs

SVGs are vector based (math created), so they can mathematically scale up or down to any dimension without loss of quality, keep in mind text is space heavy because it requires an outline
